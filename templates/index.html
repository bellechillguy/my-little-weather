About My Little Weather

A little weather page for Bandung, inspired by personal websites of the early 2000s and Equestria’s two sky keepers.

Current weather comes from OpenWeather and refreshes every 5 minutes while the page is open. The report also shows when the observations were recorded.

Auto mode switches between Celestia and Luna with Bandung’s sunrise and sunset. If those times aren’t available yet, daytime runs from 06:00 to 18:00 local time.
